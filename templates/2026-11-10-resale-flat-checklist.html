BUYER TIP

Buying a resale flat?
Check these 5 first.

रीसेल फ्लैट खरीदने से पहले ज़रूर जाँचें
1
Full title chain
All past sale deeds – up to the original allotment
2
No loan or legal dispute
Encumbrance search + bank NOC if the flat is mortgaged
3
Approved map & completion
Sanctioned building plan and completion / occupancy certificate
4
Society NOC & dues
No pending maintenance; transfer allowed by society
5
Tax & bills cleared
Latest property tax, water & electricity receipts
