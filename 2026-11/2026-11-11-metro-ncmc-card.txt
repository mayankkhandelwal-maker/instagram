Indore Metro just got cheaper for daily riders 🚇💳

From 12 November, Bhopal & Indore Metro start selling NCMC cards ("MP Metro – One Nation One Card") at station ticket counters.
✅ One-time card cost: ₹50
✅ 25% off on journeys above ₹20 – e.g. a ₹40 ride comes down to ₹30
✅ Recharge at counters, ticket machines or the MP Metro app – and the card works on other NCMC metros too

Why it matters for home buyers: as the metro becomes an easy everyday habit, living near the corridor gets even more practical – worth keeping in mind when you compare locations along Super Corridor and beyond. 🏡

Source: Metro Rail News, 11 Nov 2026
