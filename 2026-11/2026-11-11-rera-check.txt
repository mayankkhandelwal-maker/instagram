Booking a flat in Indore? Do this 2-minute check first 🔍

1️⃣ Search the project on rera.mp.gov.in – no registration, no booking
2️⃣ Match the RERA number on the brochure, ads and agreement
3️⃣ Read what the builder has declared – completion date, approved plans, progress
4️⃣ Under RERA, a builder can't take more than 10% of the price before a registered agreement for sale

A few minutes on the RERA site can save years of stress. 💡
