📰 Indore property update: IDA is getting strict on dues, and a property fair is on the way!

At a review meeting this week, the Indore Development Authority (IDA) decided to send notices to leaseholders with pending lease rent and compounding fees across its schemes. Where lease conditions have been violated, cancellation proceedings may follow.

🏠 Good news for buyers: IDA also plans to speed up the sale of its vacant properties and will organise a property fair soon.

✅ IDA plot/flat owner? Check your lease-rent status and clear any pending dues in time.
✅ Planning to buy? Keep your budget and documents ready for the fair.
