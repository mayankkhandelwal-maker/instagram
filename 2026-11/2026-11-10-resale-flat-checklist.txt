Found the perfect resale flat in Indore? 🏠 Check these 5 things before you pay the token amount.

1️⃣ Full title chain – every past sale deed, right back to the original allotment
2️⃣ No loan or dispute – run an encumbrance search; if the seller has a home loan, get the bank's NOC & loan-closure letter
3️⃣ Approved map & completion – sanctioned building plan and completion/occupancy certificate
4️⃣ Society NOC – no pending maintenance, and the society allows the transfer
5️⃣ Tax & bills – latest property tax, water and electricity receipts in order

A good resale deal in Vijay Nagar, Nipania or AB Road stays good only when the papers are clean. When in doubt, get a lawyer to verify the documents before registry. 💡
